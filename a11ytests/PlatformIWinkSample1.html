Websites

Product

An accessible and secure website, quickly online. With a custom design for your organisation and a user-friendly CMS so you can get started right away.

website packages

A custom website based on years of experience

We make custom websites, but we don't like to reinvent the wheel. Thanks to more than 20 years of experience, we know what works best online. We have developed website packages that allow you to get online quickly with the very best technology. We can customise the website completely for your organisation. And you can easily expand the website with a wide selection of modules.

development & support

A passionate team

We provide extensive support on our products and options for customisation and links with back-office systems. In addition, we continuously develop our existing packages. Our customers thus benefit from the innovations. Our consultants optimise the online strategy of your organisation together with you.

Our USP's

Why iWink?

Safety & regulation

We are ISO27001 certified and host all our sites in the Netherlands. We have extensive expertise in the field of AVG/GDPR.

Accessibility

Building accessible websites is one of our spearheads. We believe it is important that everyone has equal access to information.

Customisation

If our existing solutions are not sufficient for your needs, our specialists provide a website that is perfectly customised.

User-friendly CMS

Our CMS is described as one of the most user-friendly in the market. Very convenient if you often have to make content adjustments.
