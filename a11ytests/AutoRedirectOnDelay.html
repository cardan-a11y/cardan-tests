Understanding the Meta Refresh Tag

This page automatically redirects to another page after 10 seconds.

This approach is often used when you access an expired page or you end a cycle and there is no purpose for you to stay on this page any more.

How the Meta Refresh Works

When to Use Meta Refresh

  • Displaying real-time updates, such as live sports scores or stock prices.
  • Redirecting users after a specified amount of time (e.g., for temporary maintenance pages).
  • Refreshing dashboards or monitoring tools where auto-updating data is needed.

Considerations for Accessibility

Meta refresh can sometimes be disorienting for users, especially for those relying on assistive technologies. It's important to inform users that the page will refresh automatically and provide alternatives if possible.
