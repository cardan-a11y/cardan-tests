Understanding SVG Accessibility in Web Design

SVG (Scalable Vector Graphics) is a powerful tool for creating vector images on the web. However, ensuring that SVGs are accessible is important for users who rely on screen readers and other assistive technologies. In this blog post, we explore various ways to enhance the accessibility of SVG elements using attributes like `title`, `aria-label`, `aria-hidden`, and `role="presentation"`.
