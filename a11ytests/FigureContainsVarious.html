Welcome to our blog post discussing the role of images and captions in web content. Below, you will find examples of figures, captions, and how they can be used effectively to enhance the presentation of images or other media.

Images combined with captions help provide additional context and are particularly useful for accessibility, allowing users to better understand the content. However, captions can also exist outside of figures, offering independent descriptions or details that might not directly relate to a specific image.

Captions and figures should be carefully arranged to ensure clarity and improve the overall presentation of your content. Empty captions, while technically valid, may cause confusion and should be avoided unless necessary for specific design or accessibility purposes.
